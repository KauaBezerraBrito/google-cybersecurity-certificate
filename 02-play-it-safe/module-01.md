# Module 01 - Security Domains

## Security and Risk Management

Defining security goals and objectives, compliance requirements, and legal regulations.

## Asset Security

Protecting digital and physical assets.

## Security Architecture and Engineering

Designing and optimizing security through effective tools, systems, and processes.

## Communication and Network Security

Managing and securing physical networks and wireless communications.

## Identity and Access Management

Focused on authentication, access, and authorization to keep data secure.

Principle of Least Privilege: The concept of granting users only the minimum access and permissions required to complete a task.

## Security Assessment and Testing

Conducting tests to evaluate the effectiveness of security controls.

## Security Operations

Monitoring systems, detecting threats, and conducting security investigations.

## Software Development Security

Using secure coding practices to protect software from vulnerabilities.

## Threat, Risk, and Vulnerability

Threat: Anything that has the potential to negatively impact an organization's assets.

Risk: The potential for a threat to exploit a vulnerability and negatively impact the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of an asset.

Vulnerability: A weakness that can be exploited by a threat.

## Ransomware

A malicious attack in which threat actors encrypt an organization's data and demand payment to restore access.
