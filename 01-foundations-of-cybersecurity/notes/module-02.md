# Module 02 - The Evolution of Cybersecurity

## Malware

Software designed to harm devices or networks.

## Worm

A type of malware that can spread automatically across devices and networks without requiring user interaction.

## BEC

Business Email Compromise.

## USB Baiting

A social engineering technique in which a threat actor strategically leaves an infected USB drive for someone to find and use, potentially infecting a system or network.

## Watering Hole Attack

An attack in which a threat actor compromises a legitimate website frequently visited by a specific group or organization to target its users.

## Social Engineering Principles

- Authority
- Intimidation
- Consensus/Social proof
- Scarcity
- Familiarity
- Trust
- Urgency

## Incident Response

When a security breach occurs, one of the first priorities is to contain the breach and limit its potential impact.

## CSIRT

Computer Security Incident Response Team.

## CISSP

Certified Information Systems Security Professional.

## CISSP Security Domains

- Security and Risk Management
- Asset Security
- Security Architecture and Engineering
- Communication and Network Security
- Identity and Access Management
- Security Assessment and Testing
- Security Operations
- Software Development Security

## Threat Actors and Hackers

Threat actors are generally defined by their malicious intent, while hackers are defined by their technical skills and motivations.
